# 🍽️ Restaurant Web Development & Digital Solutions

Hi, I'm a freelance web developer specializing in high-converting, mobile-first websites for restaurants, cafes, and food service businesses. I build custom digital experiences designed to showcase your culinary brand, boost reservations, and drive direct online orders—saving you money on third-party commission fees.

---

## 🛠️ What I Build

* **Custom Restaurant Websites:** Beautiful, responsive sites built with clean code and high-performance UX.
* **Interactive Digital Menus:** Dynamic, easy-to-read menus with filterable dietary options (vegan, gluten-free, allergens).
* **Online Ordering & POS Integrations:** Direct ordering setups (Toast, ChowNow, WooCommerce) and seamless reservation embeds (OpenTable, Resy).
* **Local SEO & Schema Markup:** Structured menu data and local search optimization so customers find you first on Google.
* **Content Management Systems:** Easy-to-manage backends (WordPress, Webflow) allowing staff to update daily specials in seconds.
